# Data Purpose Boundary Design

## Status

Approved and hardened for implementation on 2026-09-06.

## Purpose

JPV-OS must prevent personal data collected, received, inferred, resolved, enriched, derived, or transformed for one legitimate purpose from silently becoming usable for a materially different purpose merely because a vendor, platform, institution, government actor, data broker, analytics provider, identity service, intermediary, affiliate, model, or downstream partner can technically connect the records.

This design converts existing People Protection privacy, consent, data-minimization, anti-surveillance, and institutional-misuse principles into enforceable data-lineage, purpose-boundary, anti-circumvention, and verification requirements.

The governing rules are:

1. Technical linkability does not create permission.
2. Unverifiable data handling is unauthorized data handling.
3. A prohibited use remains prohibited when performed indirectly.
4. Derivation, pseudonymization, inference, outsourcing, relabeling, or product restructuring cannot launder a prohibited purpose into an allowed one.
5. There is no UNKNOWN -> ALLOW state for protected data.

## Scope

This applies across JayPVentures LLC enterprise infrastructure, jaypventures creator and public-facing systems, jaypVLabs research and validation systems, JPV-OS governance/entitlement/identity/routing/orchestration layers, and all vendors, subprocessors, contractors, partners, APIs, data brokers, analytics products, identity products, fraud products, enrichment products, government-facing integrations, affiliates, acquisitions, replacements, and future equivalents.

The rule applies whether data is collected directly, obtained from a vendor, inferred, purchased, licensed, matched, resolved, enriched, pseudonymized, hashed, embedded, scored, modeled, received through legal process, or generated by automation.

## Core Invariant

Every personal-data flow must declare and preserve a bounded purpose. Cross-domain reuse is denied by default.

A dataset or derived record may not be used for a materially different purpose unless the new use has a documented legal and policy basis, satisfies People Protection and Security requirements, is minimum-necessary, preserves applicable rights, and passes the required review gate.

No actor may obtain indirectly what the actor would be prohibited from obtaining directly.

## Verification States

Every vendor or governed processing path involving protected data must resolve to exactly one state:

- VERIFIED — sufficient evidence establishes the permitted data boundary. Processing is permitted only within that verified boundary.
- QUARANTINED — evidence is incomplete, stale, contradictory, or materially changed. Protected-data release is suspended pending verification.
- DENIED — required evidence cannot be established, the vendor refuses verification, or the processing conflicts with JPV requirements. Protected-data processing is prohibited.

UNKNOWN is never an authorization state.

## No Founder or Administrative Override

No founder, administrator, executive, partner, customer, vendor, investor, or emergency operator may convert QUARANTINED, DENIED, UNKNOWN, stale, or unverifiable handling into VERIFIED by discretionary override.

A failed evidence gate can be cleared only by supplying the missing evidence and satisfying the same objective verification requirements that caused the failure. Emergency authority may address immediate continuity or safety only where separately lawful and may not waive the protected-data evidence threshold.

## Heightened-Risk Domains

Heightened-risk domains include healthcare/claims/treatment/pharmacy/eligibility/benefits/payer/provider/care-management data; identity proofing/resolution/persistent identifiers/biometrics/relationship graphs; financial/credit/banking/payment/debt/asset/income data; employment/workforce/payroll/applicant/performance data; precise or persistent location; communications metadata/contact graphs; education/student data; child/minor data; civic/voter/political participation/immigration/law-enforcement/watchlist/government-investigative data; behavioral profiles and predictive/risk/fraud scores; and combinations that materially increase identifiability, surveillance capability, exclusion risk, or adverse-decision capability.

## Derivation and Re-identification Boundary

Purpose restrictions follow the information, not its representation.

Hashes, tokens, pseudonymous identifiers, embeddings, feature vectors, inferred attributes, risk scores, similarity scores, relationship graphs, household graphs, model outputs, synthetic labels, aggregates, and nominally de-identified data remain governed when they can reasonably be linked to a person, household, device, account, organization, or protected cohort, or when they can materially affect a person.

A transformation cannot erase the original purpose restriction merely by removing a name or changing the storage format.

Re-identification, linkage, singling-out, reconstruction, or identity resolution requires explicit authorization and safeguards. Deliberately separating linkage steps across multiple services does not evade this requirement.

## Anti-Intermediary Laundering

A prohibited JPV use remains prohibited when performed through a vendor, subcontractor, affiliate, reseller, broker, clean room, customer, government partner, research partner, API chain, model provider, data exchange, or subsequent recipient.

JPV-OS must evaluate the complete reachable data path rather than only the directly contracted counterparty.

A recipient may not instruct another party to perform a prohibited query, join, inference, enrichment, match, or classification on its behalf.

## Purpose-Label Integrity

Purpose authorization is determined by actual function, inputs, outputs, affected people, and reasonably foreseeable use — not merely the label supplied by an operator.

Renaming surveillance as fraud prevention, safety, integrity, analytics, research, personalization, identity assurance, risk management, public records, intelligence, or another nominal category does not alter the applicable boundary when the substantive capability or effect is the same.

## Prohibited Cross-Domain Uses

Unless narrow lawful compulsory process requires a specific disclosure, protected data may not silently cross into a materially unrelated heightened-risk domain.

Prohibited-by-default transitions include healthcare administration to immigration enforcement, unrelated law-enforcement intelligence, political/civic profiling, advertising, generalized commercial enrichment, employment screening, or generalized watchlisting; identity proofing to unrelated behavioral surveillance; location data to generalized tracking outside the stated purpose; consumer enrichment to adverse employment/housing/insurance/immigration/civic/public-benefit decisions without an independently valid basis and review; fraud prevention to generalized watchlisting or unrelated enforcement; and compelled government disclosure to unrestricted downstream reuse.

## Lawful Compelled Disclosure

Lawful compulsory process does not dissolve the purpose boundary.

Each compelled disclosure must record requesting authority, legal basis, date received, scope requested, scope actually disclosed, minimization, authorizing reviewer, recipient, retention/preservation requirement when known, notice status, and whether subsequent use beyond the compelled purpose is actually authorized.

The default for unrelated subsequent use is no.

## Exception Containment

Emergency, fraud, security, legal-process, research, public-record, abuse-prevention, and continuity exceptions must be narrow, attributable, purpose-specific, minimum-necessary, time-bound where possible, independently reviewable, logged, and incapable of silently becoming standing authority.

An exception must not create a reusable entitlement for a different actor, dataset, purpose, or future event.

Repeated exceptions indicating a recurring operational need require formal policy review rather than indefinite exception renewal.

## Vendor Capability Review

Vendors are evaluated by capability and actual data path, not reputation, marketing, or product name. Review must address accessible source datasets, persistent identifiers, identity resolution, enrichment sources, derived attributes/scoring, relationship graphs, biometrics, investigative search, APIs/exports, subprocessors, affiliates, government/law-enforcement/intelligence/healthcare/financial/advertising/employment/data-broker lines, contractual purpose limitations, deletion/correction, configurable sharing/retention, technical isolation, and evidence supporting claimed separation.

A privacy policy, sales assurance, product name, or unsupported assertion of business-unit separation is insufficient evidence.

## Evidence Standard

Verification evidence should be independently checkable to the maximum technically and legally available extent. Depending on the integration, evidence may include architecture/data-flow documentation, contractual restrictions, subprocessor inventories, configuration evidence, signed attestations, access and egress logs, workload identity, cryptographic signatures, tamper-evident records, retention/deletion evidence, API scopes, technical isolation evidence, audit reports, and reproducible tests.

Evidence must have provenance, date, scope, and an identified issuer. Materially stale evidence cannot sustain VERIFIED status.

## Provable JPV Data Sovereignty

For infrastructure JPV governs, the target authorization chain is:

request -> authenticated workload/actor -> authorized purpose -> minimum necessary data -> governed computation -> constrained output -> tamper-evident authorization receipt.

A valid authorization receipt must bind the actor/workload identity, policy version, purpose, requested data classes, decision, time, recipient, applicable constraints, and audit reference.

Absence of a valid receipt means the operation was not an authorized JPV operation.

This provides proof of what JPV permitted or denied. It does not falsely claim omniscience over independently acquired or secretly retained data outside infrastructure JPV can observe or legally audit.

## Machine-Readable Data Flow Declaration

Systems processing personal data must include a declaration under `governance/data-flows/` using a deterministic JSON schema.

Required fields include `system_id`, `owner`, `surface`, `data_classes`, `source`, `original_purpose`, `allowed_purposes`, `prohibited_purposes`, `legal_basis`, `vendors`, `recipients`, `persistent_identifiers`, `identity_resolution`, `enrichment`, `derived_data`, `retention`, `deletion_correction_path`, `cross_domain_transfer`, `compelled_disclosure_path`, `audit_log`, `verification_state`, `evidence_refs`, `reviewed_by`, and `review_date`.

Cross-domain transfers additionally require source domain, destination domain, exact fields, justification, minimization, authorization, safeguards, expiration/review date, and downstream-use constraints.

Missing fields fail validation.

## High-Impact Vendor Registry

A machine-readable registry under `governance/vendors/` classifies vendors by capabilities rather than reputation. Heightened review is triggered by identity resolution, cross-dataset enrichment, biometric matching, persistent person identifiers, fraud/risk scoring, relationship graphs, government investigative search, healthcare aggregation, large-scale consumer data brokerage, precise location aggregation, adverse-decision support, or broad downstream API access.

The registry is a mandatory review trigger, not a blacklist. It must support LexisNexis Risk Solutions, Palantir, Experian, TransUnion, Equifax, ID-verification providers, healthcare aggregators, and future equivalents without making policy depend on any one company.

## Healthcare Boundary

Healthcare data receives explicit heightened protection because claims, eligibility, provider, pharmacy, diagnosis, treatment, utilization, care-management, and member identity data can become unusually powerful when joined with commercial identity-resolution and public-record systems.

Healthcare-purpose declarations must enumerate every non-care/non-administrative downstream use. Healthcare data is blocked by default from unrelated policing, immigration, political, advertising, employment, generalized consumer enrichment, and watchlist uses. Healthcare vendors with identity-resolution, public-record, consumer-data, or government-investigative capabilities require high-impact review. Compelled disclosures remain scoped and independently logged.

## Product, Acquisition, and Capability Drift

Purpose boundaries survive vendor acquisitions, mergers, product renames, new APIs, new subprocessors, affiliate restructuring, executive changes, government contract expansion, new customer segments, and platform consolidation.

A material capability change that could alter linkage, recipient access, secondary use, government access, enrichment, scoring, retention, or technical isolation automatically moves the affected path to QUARANTINED until re-verification completes.

## Detection, Containment, and Remedy

Detection is not terminal.

When unauthorized or unverifiable propagation is detected, JPV-OS must, within its legal and technical authority:

1. contain the affected flow;
2. suspend or revoke affected access;
3. preserve evidence;
4. identify affected data and recipients;
5. issue downstream deletion/correction/cessation requests where applicable;
6. notify affected people when appropriate and lawful;
7. escalate vendor or institutional breach/remediation processes;
8. determine whether contractual, regulatory, legal, security, or public-interest escalation is warranted;
9. verify remediation rather than accepting unsupported assurances;
10. produce a terminal incident receipt identifying what was proven, what remains externally unverifiable, and what protective action was completed.

## Continuous Verification and Adversarial Testing

Production verification must attempt circumvention, not merely validate happy paths.

Required regression cases include splitting a prohibited join across two vendors; using an affiliate as intermediary; replacing raw attributes with scores or embeddings; retaining a stable linkage key while claiming anonymization; relabeling surveillance as fraud or safety; adding a new subprocessor; acquiring a vendor with a different data estate; chaining APIs to reconstruct a prohibited dataset; using model inference to recreate a prohibited attribute; using emergency authority as standing access; using legal-process access as generalized downstream authorization; and operating without a current verification receipt.

Every such case must converge on DENIED or QUARANTINED unless the complete evidence and authorization requirements are independently satisfied.

## Enforcement Model

The deterministic validator will fail when required fields are absent; allowed/prohibited purposes conflict; heightened-risk processing lacks review; identity resolution/enrichment/derivation lacks purpose and safeguards; cross-domain transfer lacks authorization/minimization/downstream constraints/review date; a personal-data system lacks a declaration; a high-impact vendor lacks capability review; compelled disclosure is treated as blanket reuse; retention is undefined; deletion/correction is absent without justification; audit evidence is missing; verification state is UNKNOWN; VERIFIED lacks sufficient current evidence; QUARANTINED or DENIED is configured to release protected data; or policy language is weakened.

## Auditability

For any governed flow JPV must be able to determine where data came from; original purpose; permitted users; actual governed recipients; transformations/enrichment; identity linkage; purpose changes; exception authorization; expiration/review; correction/deletion path; verification state; supporting evidence; and the receipt proving the authorization decision.

## Production Gate

A personal-data system is not production-ready unless its purpose boundary is declared, validated, reviewable, and VERIFIED.

No vendor, institution, government actor, customer, partner, administrator, founder, acquisition, product rename, API expansion, intermediary, derived representation, or operational convenience may convert technical access into permission for an undeclared or unverifiable secondary use.

## Success Criteria

The change is complete only when the doctrine is binding; machine-readable data-flow/vendor schemas exist; deterministic enforcement exists; anti-circumvention regression tests pass; existing policy enforcement requires the new artifacts; People Protection/Security/Governance/production review/policy index/enforcement map/ownership routing reference the invariant; protected-data release fails closed for QUARANTINED, DENIED, UNKNOWN, or stale evidence; no discretionary override bypass exists; and a PR exposes the complete change for independent review.
